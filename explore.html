Resources & Opportunities.

Discover our digital platforms, learning resources, and how we are contributing to global goals.

Digital platform

AMYLI aims to develop a digital platform that makes mentorship, learning, research development and personal growth more accessible and coordinated.

The proposed platform will bring together participant profiles, mentor matching, personalised development plans, educational resources, research support, and progress monitoring. This centralized hub will allow users to track their growth, connect with the right people, and access materials tailored to their specific needs, regardless of their geographical location.

Learning resources

Explore the learning themes at the heart of AMYLI's work. This section will feature approved articles, stories, guides and educational resources as they become available.

We believe in open access to knowledge. Here, you will find a growing library of materials covering topics from study strategies to career readiness. Whether you are looking for a quick guide on writing a CV or a deep dive into research methodologies, our resources are designed to support self-paced learning and empower you to take charge of your development.

Global Goals Contribution

AMYLI's activities align with several United Nations Global Goals. We focus on SDG 4 (Quality Education), SDG 8 (Decent Work), SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities), SDG 16 (Peace & Justice), SDG 17 (Partnerships), and SDG 3 (Good Health).

By aligning our work with these global objectives, we ensure that our local actions contribute to a broader, sustainable impact. We measure our success not just by the number of participants, but by how effectively we are helping to build a more equitable and educated world, fostering a generation that is aware of its role in global society.
