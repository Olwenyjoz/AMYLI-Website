WHO WE ARE

Building knowledgeable, confident and responsible leaders.

AMYLI (Academic Mentorship & Youth Leadership Initiative) is a youth-focused, non-profit and non-political initiative dedicated to empowering students, young professionals, aspiring researchers, and emerging leaders through academic mentorship, education, personal development, research support, leadership training, and community engagement.

We believe that every young person deserves access to guidance, opportunities, knowledge, and supportive networks that enable them to realise their potential. Through mentorship, educational programmes, webinars, workshops, research activities, and collaborative partnerships, AMYLI helps young people navigate academic life, develop practical skills, build meaningful connections, and prepare for successful professional and personal futures.

Discover our mission ↗
OUR MISSION

Empowering people through learning.

To empower students and young professionals through accessible academic mentorship, education, research development, leadership training, and community engagement, enabling them to reach their full potential and contribute meaningfully to society.

OUR VISION

A generation ready to lead.

To build a generation of academically strong, socially responsible, internationally minded, and confident young professionals capable of contributing positively to their communities and countries.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our core values.

Our values shape how we learn, work together, serve communities and support the next generation.

01

Academic excellence

Encouraging lifelong learning, intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and the pursuit of knowledge.

02

Mentorship and mutual support

Connecting individuals through meaningful relationships that promote guidance, encouragement, knowledge-sharing, and personal growth.

03

Youth empowerment

Equipping young people with the confidence, skills, and opportunities to take initiative and become active contributors to society.

04

Inclusion and equal opportunity

Promoting a welcoming environment where young people from different educational, cultural, linguistic, and socioeconomic backgrounds can participate and develop.

05

Leadership and responsibility

Encouraging ethical decision-making, accountability, initiative, and service-oriented leadership.

06

Research and innovation

Supporting scientific curiosity, evidence-based thinking, research skills, and the development of innovative solutions to social challenges.

07

Volunteerism and community service

Inspiring young people to use their time, knowledge, and skills to support others and improve their communities.

08

Integrity and respect

Upholding professional ethics, respect, and intercultural understanding.

09

Partnership and international cooperation

Building constructive relationships among universities, educational institutions, professional communities, civil society, and youth organisations.

OUR PROGRAMMES

Turning potential into progress.

We connect young people with knowledge, guidance, skills and supportive networks to encourage academic, professional and personal development.

✦

Academic mentorship

Connecting students with experienced mentors for academic guidance.

AMYLI connects students and young professionals with experienced mentors, researchers, educators, and other suitably qualified individuals who can provide guidance based on participants' interests, goals, and development needs.
◎

Student adaptation

Helping students navigate university life and new environments.

Beginning university or studying in a new country can be a significant transition. AMYLI helps students better understand academic expectations, navigate university life, build social connections, and develop the confidence to participate actively in their educational communities.
♡

Health & wellbeing

Promoting mental well-being, stress management, and healthy habits.

We recognise that academic success and personal growth are closely connected to physical health, mental well-being, emotional resilience, and healthy daily habits. AMYLI promotes awareness of stress management, burnout prevention, time management, healthy lifestyles, self-confidence, and work-life balance.
⌕

Research development

Strengthening academic writing, methodology, and scientific skills.

AMYLI encourages young people to develop their research interests, strengthen academic writing skills, and participate in scientific and intellectual activities.
↗

Leadership & careers

Developing practical leadership and professional skills for future careers.

AMYLI supports the development of practical leadership and professional skills that help young people transition from education into meaningful careers and community leadership.
▦

Workshops & events

Bringing together students, mentors, and professionals to exchange knowledge.

AMYLI organises educational activities designed to bring together students, mentors, academics, researchers, professionals, volunteers, and youth leaders.
◇

Community engagement

Encouraging volunteering and collaborative projects that respond to real needs.

We believe that meaningful development includes a commitment to serving others. AMYLI encourages young people to participate in volunteering, peer support, educational outreach, community projects, and collaborative activities that respond to real needs.
THE AMYLI DEVELOPMENT JOURNEY

From learning to leadership.

Our approach to youth development is guided by six interconnected stages.

01

Adapt

Understand new environments, navigate challenges, and develop the confidence to participate in academic and social communities.

02

Learn

Acquire knowledge, strengthen academic skills, and embrace continuous personal and professional development.

03

Connect

Build meaningful relationships with mentors, peers, educators, researchers, and professional networks.

04

Lead

Develop initiative, communication, teamwork, decision-making, and responsible leadership.

05

Serve

Apply knowledge and skills through volunteering, community engagement, and activities that benefit others.

06

Mentor

Share experience, support others, and contribute to a sustainable culture of guidance, learning, and mutual growth.
